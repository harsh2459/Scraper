Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TM Tracker (“we”, “us”) is a trademark portfolio tracking service available at mytmtracker.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it and the choices you have. By using TM Tracker you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and sign-in credentials (passwords are stored only in hashed form).
  • Portfolio information: the trademark application numbers you add or track, notes, tasks and reminders you create, and related details.
  • Public registry information: data about trademarks that is published by the Indian trade marks registry, such as status, class, proprietor, notices and documents, which we retrieve for the trademarks you track.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe, the name, email and billing address needed to issue invoices.
  • Technical information: basic logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your trademarks, documents, deadlines and updates, and to send alerts and reminders you have asked for.
  • To issue invoices and manage your subscription.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the service and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Google Drive

We use Google Drive, through a Google account that we operate, to store copies of trademark documents and images that we retrieve from the public registry. We use Google’s limited “drive.file” permission, which only allows the service to access files it has created itself. We do not access your personal Google Drive and we do not ask you to sign in to Google.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the service (for example hosting, email delivery, document storage and invoicing), and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if required by law. These providers must keep the information confidential and secure.

5. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and security cookies to protect your account. We do not use cookies for advertising.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example tax and accounting records). You can ask us to delete your account and portfolio data.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent for optional communications, by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

TM Tracker is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
