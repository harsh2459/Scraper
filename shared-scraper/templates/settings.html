How it works

1
Scraper hits the site

When quickcompany.in asks for email verification, the scraper detects the OTP page automatically.

2
Inbox is polled

Your inbox is checked every few seconds via IMAP (using your app password — not your real password).

3
OTP is extracted & submitted

The code is read from the email body and submitted back to the site automatically.
